PryceHunt for Chrome and Edge

Compare prices across Indian stores, right on the page

PryceHunt shows what the product you are looking at costs at other stores, how its price has moved, and whether that big discount is real. No account, no ads, no tracking: your pincode, settings and watch-list stay in your browser.

See other stores' prices without leaving the page

A small box under the price opens the comparison. Switch on “Show the price here instead of a link” and PryceHunt shows the other store's price in the box, marked “likely the same product” or “similar: check details”. You can switch that on or off right there.

  • Live prices today for Amazon.in and Flipkart; the other stores open their own search
  • The cheapest store found is called out, and you can list the lowest price first
  • Quick-delivery apps are included, with prices per 100 g, per 100 ml or per piece

A Compare button on every product

On search, category and home pages, and on the similar items of a product page, every product gets a small “Compare prices” button. Nothing is fetched until you press one.

  • Amazon.in, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, Ajio and Croma
  • Blinkit, Zepto, BigBasket and Swiggy Instamart
  • Pause it for 30 minutes to 23 hours, on one site or on all sites

Price history and honest sale facts

PryceHunt remembers the prices you have seen and charts them. It shows the lowest price seen, checks a “70% off” claim against what the product has usually cost, and during big sales such as Diwali tells you what the product cost before the sale began.

  • No verdict and no pressure: it states the facts and you decide
  • Sale dates are the usual timing, because shops announce theirs late; you can choose the seasons to follow and add your own
  • Optional shared history, built from prices other people chose to share anonymously

Your choices, in clear settings

Everything that contacts anything is off until you switch it on, and each setting says what is sent. Choose your stores and their order, where the box appears, and how it behaves.

  • No account to make
  • Live prices, alerts, price sharing and Telegram are separate switches
  • Erase everything with one button

Pause, watch and get alerts

The toolbar menu pauses PryceHunt for a while, keeps your watch-list and lets you set your pincode. Set an alert price or range for a product, choose which sites to watch during a sale, and get a notification when it drops.

  • Alerts are checked while your browser is open. Some stores, such as Amazon.in, do not allow automatic checks, so those prices update when you open the product
  • Optional alerts on Telegram, to reach your phone

Share a comparison with family and friends

Pressing Share makes a picture and a short message of the comparison, ready for WhatsApp or Telegram. It contains the product and prices only, nothing about you.

Private by design

PryceHunt has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and we never sell data. Your settings, pincode, watch-list and price history stay in your browser. A few optional features send limited information to our price server or to other services; each one is off until you switch it on, and the settings say exactly what is sent.

Good to know

  • Products are matched by name, so a match can be wrong. Always check the details before you buy, and flag a wrong match or price.
  • When you click “Go to this store”, PryceHunt may add an affiliate link that earns us a commission at no cost to you, and it says so next to the button.
  • PryceHunt is independent and is not affiliated with any store or with Telegram.

Works on

Amazon.in, Flipkart, Meesho, Myntra, Ajio, Croma, Blinkit, Zepto, BigBasket and Swiggy Instamart. More Indian stores are added over time.

Questions

Is PryceHunt free?

Yes, while it is being launched. If a paid plan with higher limits comes later, we will tell you well before anything changes.

Which browsers does it work in?

Chrome and Edge on a computer. Other browsers may come later.

Do I need an account?

No. You never sign in, and your settings stay in your browser.

How accurate are the matches?

PryceHunt matches products by name, so it can be wrong: a different size, colour or model can look the same. It labels each match as “likely the same product” or “similar: check details”, and you can flag a wrong match or price.

Does PryceHunt see what I browse?

On the shopping sites it works on, it reads the product name and price on the page you are viewing, on your computer. It sends nothing by itself. A few optional features send limited information, each only after you switch it on.

Why can't alerts arrive while my browser is closed?

The price checks run in your browser, so they need it open. You can also let PryceHunt watch for you while the browser is closed, using prices that other people chose to share, and receive alerts on Telegram; this can miss some drops.

Does PryceHunt earn money from my purchases?

It may. When you click “Go to this store” and the store is cheaper or has an offer, PryceHunt may open an affiliate link that earns us a commission at no cost to you. It says so next to the button, and the link is added only when you click.
